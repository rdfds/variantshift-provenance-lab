Main result

The additive model loses 0.37 Spearman when every test residue position is absent from training. Its nominal 80% interval coverage falls from 81.0% to 63.2%, exposing uncertainty shift as well as ranking failure.

Generalization by evaluation regime

Random variantUnseen positionHigher mutation depth

Evaluation design

Random variant

Groups identical mutation strings, preventing exact variants from crossing the boundary while allowing familiar residue positions.

Unseen position

Holds out complete residue positions. Mixed variants that bridge train and test residues are excluded.

Mutation depth

Trains on single substitutions and evaluates on variants containing two to five substitutions.
