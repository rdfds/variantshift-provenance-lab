# Research records

The [methods and results overview](PROJECT_REPORT.md) explains the project. Use the resources
below when you want to examine a study or reproduce an analysis.

## How the studies work

- [TEV methods](METHODS.md) and [ProteinGym methods](PROTEINGYM_METHODS.md): datasets, models, and evaluation rules.
- [Extended methods](EXTENDED_METHODS.md) and [research report](RESEARCH_REPORT.md): comparisons across proteins and families.
- [External validation](EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_REPORT.md), [model selection](CONSERVATIVE_AUDITOR_V2.md), and [predictor combinations](../results/predictor-span-pilot-v1/PREDICTOR_SPAN_PILOT.md): individual study findings and limitations.

## Results and supporting files

[Result tables and manifests](../results/) contain the reported measurements, input records,
and file checksums. See [getting started](GETTING_STARTED.md) for verification commands.

## Study protocols

[Protocols](../protocols/), [confirmation registration](CONFIRMATION_PREREGISTRATION_V2.md),
and the [confirmation evaluator](CONFIRMATION_EVALUATOR_V2.md) document the planned analyses.
[Research status](PUBLICATION_READINESS.md) explains what is complete and what remains unvalidated.
These supporting records retain the source identifiers needed to audit the studies.

## Technical reference

[Data formats](SCHEMAS_AND_INTERFACES.md) and [compute setup](COMPUTE_AND_RELEASE.md) cover
