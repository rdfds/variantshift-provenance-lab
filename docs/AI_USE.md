# AI assistance disclosure

AI assistance was used for software drafting, refactoring, tests, documentation, and analysis
planning. The human author is responsible for checking code, licenses, data provenance,
statistical choices, scientific interpretation, and every manuscript claim. AI systems are not
authors and are not credited as such.

No sample or invented result belongs in a manuscript. Tables and claims must be generated from
hashed artifacts at the registered code revision. Prompts or generated prose cannot override the
confirmation lock, dataset terms, preregistration, or authorship and disclosure requirements of the
submission venue.
